# Teacher experiment sequence

The active design is [teacher robustness v2](isaac_teacher_v2_experiment.md), with the machine-readable [parent protocol](../configs/sim/teacher_v2_delivery_protocol.json). It supersedes the earlier mixed-model and object-placement-first proposal, preserved in [the archive](archive/isaac_next_experiments_before_teacher_v2.md).

Collection kept the waffle approximately fixed while arm starts and trajectories varied. The primary comparison therefore fixes the canonical August22 green packet, camera, lighting and materials, and transfers ten synchronized measured arm/gripper/wrist starts. It compares teachers only. Four configurations receive four discovery starts and two seeds each; two configurations then receive six reserved starts and fresh seeds. The selection rule and physical success thresholds are fixed before scoring. Reserved starts are held out from simulator selection, not claimed unseen during training.

First finish this comparison and inspect failures by physical stage. A teacher can be a useful pickup candidate without being ready for placement. Full task requires independently observed acquisition, lift, carry, release, containment, unloading and bin support. Normal controller completion alone is insufficient. A clear simulator winner additionally needs the declared confirmation success and paired uncertainty gates; an inconclusive result is retained.

Next obtain the [setup measurements](isaac_teacher_measurements_20260907.md) and a short teacher-only physical pilot using marked packet placement and representative measured arm starts. Freeze any corrected geometry or sensor calibration as a new simulator version, validate recorded motion again, and rerun matched cases before carrying a ranking across versions. The new shared release/finish controller is opt-in and requires a measured, reviewed robot-frame release volume for the lab.

Only after that, test small packet placement errors around the marked reset pose for the selected teacher. Use the measured distribution of actual reset errors to set translation/yaw ranges; reserve new sampling seeds and keep the arm start fixed in this block. This measures tolerance to reset mistakes rather than changing the primary collection distribution. Report friction, camera and delay perturbations as separate stress blocks. Joint arm-plus-object randomization is a later factorial experiment after each factor is characterized independently.

Keep force, depth, speed and workspace limits fixed. High simulated backing/edge loads require contact-model and command-path diagnosis; they do not justify increasing safety limits or presenting non-gel contact as tactile gel load. Preserve failed trials, actual stop reasons, accepted commands, raw observations and tactile side-by-side video.
